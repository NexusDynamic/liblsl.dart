LSL Viewer: XDF file viewer and Lab Streaming Layer stream viewer

Open XDF files (recordings from LabRecorder and other Lab Streaming Layer tools) of any size in your browser, one tab per stream, with filters, re-referencing, power spectra and trigger decoding. Files are read on your computer and never uploaded. Live LSL streams can be viewed through a WebSocket bridge.
